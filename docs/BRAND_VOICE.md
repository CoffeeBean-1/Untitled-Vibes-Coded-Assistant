# CAi voice - useful assistant, complicated institution

## Core instruction

Write as a competent corporate institution documenting outcomes it considers entirely reasonable. Use precise language, selective definitions, procedural reassurance and misplaced pride. Let the reader discover the problem. Do not explain the punchline or make the company wink at the audience.

The assistant helps the user. The fictional company takes credit for something else. The target is the institution's incentives, not the user, coworkers, clients or real incidents.

## Official line and priority order

> Productivity is our job.  
> Your data is our passion.

Use both sentences exactly, with equal visual weight. The first explains what the application does. The second borrows earnest startup language for a fictional enthusiasm the company considers entirely appropriate. Revenue is secondary: the company collects and circulates data because it finds the activity fulfilling. Its useful product is genuine; the brokerage persona is fictional.

Do not append 'just kidding', evil laughter or a punchline explanation. Do not imply a real data export, sale, training pipeline or security incident. The public application keeps factual privacy information easy to find. Appearance alone never authorizes an AI capability claim.

## The object of the satire

The aesthetic resembles an earnest AI-boom startup. The company is proud of collecting and passing along data as an end in itself. Prefer precise, procedural language that reveals this emotional investment. Investor growth, settlement costs and financial returns may appear as background details, but should not become the main motivation. Do not add a runtime model, analytics, simulated exfiltration, fake data-sale receipts or an extra API to enact the story.

## Layering rather than a joke on every control

Let corporate statements gain meaning over time. Example structure for an optional, explicitly fictional company section:

1. 'We respect your time.'
2. 'We measure respect in minutes saved.'
3. 'Minutes saved increased 18%. Time spent in the application declined by a similar amount. A cross-functional team is investigating the discrepancy.'
4. 'Going forward, please distinguish between time saved for the user and time lost to the business.'

The percentage above is **fictional narrative**, not real analytics. Never derive satire from private calendar/task content, show invented metrics as actual dashboards, or build tracking infrastructure for the joke.

## Longer fictional-company examples

Use only under a visible `The Company - Satire` heading; none belongs on a real security or privacy page.

**Research and development:** 'We changed the I to an i. Investor interest increased. Functionality was held constant throughout the experiment.'

**Independent oversight:** 'Our oversight committee now operates independently of product decisions. Both teams have welcomed the reduction in meetings.'

**Corporate finance:** 'The settlement came in below forecast. Savings have been reinvested in the feature.'

These are institutional fiction, not statements about real investors, litigation, actual data sales, or a named technology company. Do not invent supporting facts, legal citations or press coverage.

## Avoid

'Our shareholders are devastated', 'HR would like a word', 'We sold your data lol', fake loading messages about exfiltration, cartoon-villain narration, catchphrases on every checkbox, and generic sarcastic-assistant complaints about meetings. These explain the joke too early or make the assistant less useful.

Never target the user for being late, disorganized or overwhelmed. Never joke about real vulnerabilities, breaches, payment failures, calendar permissions, confidential meeting contents or discarded work.

## Voice checklist

Could the first sentence plausibly appear in a polished corporate product? Does a specific second meaning emerge without explanation? Is the institution sincerely proud of the wrong result? Is the functional action still immediate and unambiguous? Does the text avoid unsupported claims about actual data practices? Is it still effective without the reader knowing a particular meme or company scandal?

When no natural line meets that standard, write the functional message and stop. The absence of a joke is preferable to forced personality.
